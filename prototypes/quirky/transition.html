One transition for every pose

The pose squeezes into the Sama logo, the logo rolls and settles, and the next pose pops out of it. It is drawn in code from the stills, so a new pose needs only its still, no video.

Mountain

How it works

  • Each pose is one still with the figure cut out. The logo is one more still, shared by all poses.
  • The figure shrinks toward the logo's spot on the mat with a little squash, the logo rolls in a quarter turn like the app's Ball transition, then the same steps run backward into the next pose.
  • Hand-drawn timing holds each drawing for two frames (12 per second) and gives the lines a slight wobble, so the motion looks drawn rather than tweened.
