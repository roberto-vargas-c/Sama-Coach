A test of a painted, image-based figure in a flat 2D scene. Every change of pose goes through the Sama logo: the figure melts into it, and the next pose grows back out of it.

How it scales

  • Each pose needs one still and one 4 second clip of it melting into the logo. The clip plays forward to leave the pose and backward to arrive in it.
  • So any pose can reach any other pose, and the cost grows with the number of poses, not with the number of pairs.
  • The mat and paper are part of the painting in this test. In the app they would come from the scene, and the figure would be cut out.
