Everything Sama can do

A yoga guide that watches your alignment through your camera, plans and talks you through practices, and shapes them around you. Free, without ads, and the camera never leaves your phone.

Check your alignment

Point your camera at the mat and Sama measures your joints against the pose, live.

Live camera, photo or clip

Check a pose live, take a photo, record a short clip or pick one from your gallery.

Joint-by-joint checks

Knees, hips, spine, shoulders and arms are each measured against a target with a fair margin, and grouped into legs, torso and arms.

A score and what to fix first

Every check gives a score, what is already in line, and the one correction that matters most.

Camera in front or beside the mat

Sama detects where your phone is and checks the pose from that side. Move it later and it notices, or set it yourself.

See yourself as your figure

The camera view can show you as your own figure, or as the video with the skeleton drawn over it.

Alignment lines

Turn on lines on the figure to see each angle Sama measures, with a small reference figure in the corner.

Runs on your phone

Two pose models (MoveNet and BlazePose, with hands and feet) run inside the browser. Choose the engine, or leave it on Auto.

Report a pose

If a pose or a check looks wrong, report it in two taps. Every report is reviewed and the pose gets fixed.

Guided practices

Plan a practice in seconds and let a calm voice take you through it.

Plan your own

Pick the length, your focus, your level and a goal pose. Tell Sama to take it easy on wrists, knees, your back or your period.

Ready-made practices

Kind to your back, Morning wake-up, Sun salutation flow, Strong and steady, Open hips, Find your balance, Evening wind-down, Yin and Ashtanga primary.

Save the ones you love

Keep your favourite practices and start them again with one tap.

Voice guide and music

A calm voice names each pose and each move between them, with soft background music. Both can be turned off.

Real movement between poses New

The figure steps, folds, squats and jumps from pose to pose, timed on filmed yoga classes. Prefer something softer? The blob melts from one pose into the next.

Calm, Flowing or Quick

Choose how fast the moves go, from slow mornings to a power flow.

Tap to move on

Tap the right of the picture for the next pose, the middle to pause and the left to go back.

Watch on your TV

Cast or mirror to a TV, or switch to the big TV layout with the camera and the timer.

Record your moves

Optionally keep your joint positions between poses (never video) to look back on, download or delete.

The pose library

165 poses, from Mountain to Firefly.

Search in English or Sanskrit

Find any pose by name, filter by family and level, from gentle to advanced.

Every angle

Turn the figure to see a pose from the front, the side or in between, and switch to the other side.

Where a pose comes from

Each pose has its Sanskrit name, what it means and a short history.

Seven places to practise

Put the mat on a plain background, in a studio, a living room, a park, on a beach, in the mountains or on a night rooftop.

Your AI coach

A coach that knows your practice, powered by GPT-6.1 Sol. It only ever reads text, never your camera.

Plan with me

Answer a few questions, one at a time, and get a practice made for you.

Review my checks

Feedback on how your alignment is going: strengths, what to work on and what to fix first.

Review a routine

Paste or pick a routine and get a corrected version with notes on what works and what is missing.

Roadmap to a pose

The weeks and the poses that build toward a pose you dream of, like Crow or a headstand.

Your calendar

A month view of your practices, practice days and cycle, right next to the coach.

Your figure, your look

The guide on the mat looks like you, everywhere in the app.

Make it yours

Skin tone, hair and hair colour, build, top, pattern, trousers or shorts, sleeves, beard and mat colour.

Your height on your mat New

Set your height and a standard (183 cm) or XL (213 cm) mat to see where you really land on it.

Colours that suit you

The background, walls and floors adjust to your skin tone so the figure always stands out.

Light or dark

Light by default, with a dark mode and an automatic option that follows your phone.

Practice that follows your cycle

Optional, and it stays on your device.

Your cycle at a glance

Log your periods, see predicted days and ovulation in the calendar, and set your cycle and period length.

Softer when you need it

On your period, practices skip inversions, deep twists and strong core work. When your energy is up, they get stronger.

Progress and planning

Your progress

Checks, best score, poses in line and minutes practised, your recent checks, and each step on the road to your goal pose.

Practice days

Pick your days and a time, and see them in Sama's calendar.

Google Calendar

Put your practice days in Google Calendar with a reminder.

Your profile

Your level, your goal pose, how often you want to practise, and notes the coach should know, like an old injury.

Private by design

The camera stays on your phone

Photos, clips and the camera stream are never uploaded. Only text reaches the coach.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner either.

Sync if you want it

Sign in with Google to keep your practice across phone, laptop and the app. Or don't, and everything stays on this device.

Your data, your call

Download everything Sama knows about you as one file, or delete it. Read what Sama stores.

On every screen

On your home screen

Add Sama to your iPhone or Android home screen and it opens like an app, no download needed.

Android app

A small Android app with the full Sama inside, including casting to your TV.

A welcome and a tour

A short welcome gets to know you, and a tour shows you around. Replay both any time.

English, Spanish and German New

The whole app, every pose and every tip in your language, picked from your phone or in Settings. The voice guide speaks English for now.

Did you know? New

Now and then a small tip points out something you may have missed, and takes you straight to it.

Try it on your mat

Sama runs in your browser. Open it on your phone, prop it up and start with a single pose.
