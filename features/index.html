Check your alignment

Your camera measures every joint

Prop up your phone, hold the pose, and Sama compares your body with it, joint by joint, as you breathe.

  • Live, photo or clip. Check a pose live, take a photo, record a short clip or pick one from your gallery.
  • A score and one thing to fix first. Knees, hips, spine, shoulders and arms, each against a fair target.
  • In front of the mat or beside it. Sama works out where your phone is, and notices when you move it.
  • See yourself as your figure, or as the video with the skeleton and alignment lines on top.
  • Spotted something off? Report a pose in two taps and it gets fixed.

Guided practices

A practice in seconds, a calm voice through it

Pick how long, what to focus on and your level. Sama builds the sequence and guides you pose by pose.

  • Nine ready-made practices, from Kind to your back to Ashtanga primary, or plan your own and save it.
  • Take it easy on wrists, knees, your back or your period.
  • Voice guide and soft music. Tap right for the next pose, the middle to pause, left to go back.
  • Calm, Flowing or Quick. Choose how fast the moves go.
  • On your TV with a big layout that keeps the camera and the timer.

Real movement between poses NEW · The figure steps, squats, folds and jumps from one pose to the next, timed on real yoga classes. Prefer something softer? A blob melts from pose to pose.

The pose library

165 poses, from Mountain to Firefly

Every pose with how to do it, what to watch for, and where it comes from.

  • Search in English or Sanskrit, filter by family and by level, from gentle to advanced.
  • Any angle. Turn the figure to see a pose from the front, the side or in between, and switch sides.
  • A short history of every pose, its Sanskrit name and what it means.

Your coach

A coach that knows your practice

Powered by GPT-6.1 Sol. It reads your answers and your scores, never your camera.

  • Plan with me. A few questions, one at a time, and a practice made for you.
  • Review my checks. Strengths, what to work on, what to fix first.
  • Review a routine. Paste one in and get a corrected version with notes.
  • Roadmap to a pose. The weeks and steps toward a pose you dream of, like Crow or a headstand.

Your figure, your look

The guide on the mat looks like you

Skin tone, hair, build, clothes, beard and mat colour. Set your height and a standard or XL mat to see where you really land.

Your cycle and your week

Practice that follows your cycle

Optional, and it stays on your device. On your period, practices skip inversions, deep twists and strong core work; when your energy is up, they get stronger.

  • Your cycle at a glance, with predicted days and ovulation in the calendar.
  • Practice days and a time, and Google Calendar with a reminder.
  • Your progress: scores, minutes practised, recent checks and each step on the road to your goal pose.
